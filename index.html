Each rung holds an equal slice and matures in sequence. As a rung matures it refills checking (or rolls forward), so cash keeps surfacing without ever selling a security early.

② 24-month bear-market buffer

Buffer glide path —

Sequence-of-returns risk — a crash early in retirement doing lasting damage — peaks in the decade around your retirement date. The cushion you need isn't constant: lighter while you're still earning, heaviest at the handoff, easing later.

③ Q4 cash-conversion schedule

Every fourth quarter, move the next calendar year's full spending into cash. Anything you'll spend within 12 months is already in cash before the year starts — so you never have to sell into a down market.
